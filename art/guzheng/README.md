# Guzheng samples

Real recorded guzheng plucks (34 notes, D2-D6, two dynamics, round robins), sliced and tuned by sparklecatta-lang
(https://github.com/sparklecatta-lang/XSXB-Band-Samples, guzheng.sfz) from the Freesound recording
"LOVELY CHINESE GUZHENG PLUCKED" by Pufermufin (https://freesound.org/people/Pufermufin/sounds/396868/).
Both are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): free for commercial use, no attribution required (credited anyway).
Processed for Cipher Path: mono, leading silence removed, 3.6 s max with a fade, MP3 56 kbit/s. `t` = tuning in cents, `db` = level.
The strings are tuned D F G A C, so the music is written in F gong (宫) pentatonic.
