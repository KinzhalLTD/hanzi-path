Support

Questions, a wrong answer in a quiz, or a lab that will not pass? Email support@kinzhal.space with your iPhone model and what you tapped. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

Restore PRO on a new phone: Me → Restore purchases.
Progress: stored on this device only; it is not synced between devices.
Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
